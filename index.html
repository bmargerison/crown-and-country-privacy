Crown & Country Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Crown & Country respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Crown & Country Android application handles information.

Information we collect

Crown & Country does not require you to create an account.

The app does not currently ask you to provide your name, email address, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or other personal information.

The app does not currently contain advertising or user tracking.

Information sent to our server

When you use Crown & Country, the app connects to our server to retrieve quiz questions and related country information.

As with normal internet communication, requests to our server may include technical information necessary to establish and process the connection, such as an IP address, request information, and basic connection information.

This information is used to provide and maintain the service, troubleshoot technical problems, and protect the service against misuse.

Crown & Country does not use this information to create a personal user profile.

How we use information

Information processed when the app communicates with our server is used to:

  • provide quiz questions and country information;
  • operate and maintain the service;
  • diagnose technical problems; and
  • protect the service against misuse.

Sharing of information

Crown & Country does not sell personal information.

The Crown & Country backend is hosted using Microsoft Azure. Information transmitted between the app and our server is protected using HTTPS encryption.

We do not currently share user information with advertisers or advertising networks.

Data retention and deletion

Crown & Country does not currently maintain user accounts or user profiles.

Because the app does not currently provide account creation, there is no user account or user profile for a user to delete.

Technical information associated with requests to the service may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service, after which it may be deleted or aggregated.

Children's privacy

Crown & Country is not specifically directed at children.

If the intended audience or features of the app change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available at the public Privacy Policy URL associated with Crown & Country.
